LMC Utils

LMC Utils is a browser-based toolkit for reasoning about automata and formal languages. The algorithms implemented here follow the textbook treatment in Introduction to the Theory of Computation by Michael Sipser.

Finite automata

  • Convert an NFA to an equivalent regular expression.
  • Check equivalence of two regular expressions.

Pushdown automata

  • Normalize a context-free grammar to Chomsky normal form (CNF).
  • Check membership of a string in a CFG.
  • Convert a PDA to an equivalent CFG.
  • Check (in)equivalence of two CFGs.
